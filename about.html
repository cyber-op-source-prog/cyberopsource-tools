Security for the Rest of Us

Cyber Op Source was built on a simple idea: Security tools shouldn't be a trap.

Our Mission

In the cybersecurity world, small and medium businesses (SMBs) are often left behind. You’re too big to be ignored by hackers, but often too small to afford a $200k/year security team.

Most "free" tools on the internet are just data-harvesting machines. When you upload a file or paste sensitive data into a random converter, you’re giving your business secrets away to a stranger. We think that's wrong.

🛡️ No Judgment

There are no "stupid" questions here. We provide tools and education in plain English so you can protect what you’ve built.

🔐 Client-Side Only

Our tools run entirely in your browser. Your data never leaves your computer. We can't see it, and we don't want to.

📂 Open Source

Transparency is the foundation of trust. Our code is available for anyone to audit on GitHub. No hidden backdoors.

Why "Cyber Op Source"?

We combine Operations (practical, real-world tools) with Open Source (transparency and community). Our goal is to be your "Blue Team"—the defenders who help you harden your business against modern threats without the jargon or the high price tag.
